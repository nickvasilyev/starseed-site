Starseed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy describes how Starseed (the "app"), published by Moonlite Games, handles your information.

Information we collect

Starseed does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information or usage data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (cleared sectors, stars, best times) and settings (music, sound effects and difficulty) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Purchases

Starseed offers an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full campaign. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app asks Google Play only whether your account owns the purchase, so it can unlock the content and restore it on a new device. That check happens on your device, and nothing is sent to us.

Third parties

Starseed does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Google Play may collect information as part of downloading, updating and buying apps, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

Children

Starseed does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, for example if online features are added, we will update this page and the date above.
